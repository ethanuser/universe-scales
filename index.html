About Universe Scales

An interactive atlas of scale: compare lengths, areas, volumes, durations and dozens of other quantities, from the Planck length to the observable universe.

Created by
Ethan Nguyen · version 1.3
Inspiration
The Scale of the Universe by Cary and Michael Huang
3D models
NASA, the Smithsonian, scientific datasets and Sketchfab creators under open licenses; each model's author and license appear with it in the explorer.
Images
Wikimedia Commons, NASA and Unsplash; each item links its source.
Music
"Frozen Star" by Kevin MacLeod
Source code
github.com/ethanuser/universe-scales

Note on scale: These visualizations use logarithmic scales to bridge vast differences. Physical dimensions are often complex; for example, "size" can refer to radius, diameter, or wavelength. This tool provides general comparisons for intuition.
