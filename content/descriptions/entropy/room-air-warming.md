summary_short: Air responds lightly to heating, so a roomful of it changes entropy
  far less than the same mass of water.

Air is easy to overlook because it is invisible, but a roomful of it still has thermal memory. Heating it by one degree moves entropy in a way that feels smaller than water, which is part of why air warms and cools so quickly.
