This is not a physical wall but the area of our cosmic horizon, set by the fact that light has had only a finite time to reach us. The radius is about $46$ billion light-years, so the oldest [cosmic microwave background](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmic_microwave_background) light and every galaxy we can ever map both live inside the same observable shell. On this scale, area becomes a statement about access to information, not just geometry.
