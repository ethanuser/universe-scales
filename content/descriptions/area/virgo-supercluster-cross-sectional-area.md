By the time you reach supercluster scale, even rich galaxy clusters stop feeling like worlds and start feeling like nodes in a loose web. This area is the footprint of large-scale structure: the kind of scale on which the Milky Way, Andromeda, and Virgo cluster all count as local detail rather than the main event.
