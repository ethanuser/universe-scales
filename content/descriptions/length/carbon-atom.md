The 70 pm marker is carbon's **empirical atomic radius**, a useful comparison number rather than an edge you could touch. Carbon's nonbonded, van der Waals radius is about 177 pm, so the two conventions answer different questions. In the explorer, the blue inner cloud represents its 1s electrons; the outer 2s cloud and two perpendicular, dumbbell-shaped 2p clouds show where its remaining four electrons are likely to be found. The clouds use an independent-electron approximation, not an exact six-electron solution. [WebElements lists both radii](https://webelements.co.uk/carbon/atom_sizes.html).
