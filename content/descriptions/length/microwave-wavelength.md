Most home microwave ovens operate near 2.45 GHz. In free space, $\lambda=c/f$ gives a wavelength of about 12.2 cm, on the scale of an oven cavity rather than a molecule of food. The appliance heats food through how its alternating field interacts with matter; this marker measures one cycle of the field **in free space**. Inside food, the wavelength is shorter because the wave travels more slowly. [FDA identifies 2.45 GHz as the home-oven frequency](https://www.fda.gov/media/103619/download).
