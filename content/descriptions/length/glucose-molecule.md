Glucose is not one rigid shape. In water, its carbon chain usually closes into a six-membered ring, which puckers into a chair rather than lying flat. The 3D explorer chooses [alpha-D-glucopyranose](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/79025), one specific anomer and conformer, so its visible hydroxyl groups have definite positions. The 1 nm marker is a round-number molecular span, not the distance between neighboring atoms; individual C–C and C–O bonds are much shorter.
