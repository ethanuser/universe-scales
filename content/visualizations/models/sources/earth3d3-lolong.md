# Saltwater crocodile at Lolong's length

Original CC0 anatomical illustration by Universe Scales. Rebuild offline with
`scripts/build_earth3d3_models.py crocodile`.

- [Guinness: largest crocodile in captivity ever](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/117291-largest-crocodile-in-captivity-ever)
  identifies Lolong as Crocodylus porosus and gives 6.17 m total length.
- [Guinness's 2013 account](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2013/2/worlds-largest-crocodile-in-captivity-dies)
  describes the verification of that measurement.
- [Australian Museum: Estuarine crocodile](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/reptiles/estuarine-crocodile/):
  broad snout, heavy adult build, dorsal eyes/nostrils, embedded osteoderms,
  short limbs, muscular tail, adult grey/tan/dark coloration and a cream belly.
  Its photographs were consulted, not copied.

The only specimen-specific constraint is Lolong's 6.17 m nose-to-tail extent.
The geometry is an authored generic adult saltwater crocodile, not a scan or
reconstruction of Lolong. Head size, body girth, tail cross-sections, limb pose,
scute count, colors and exposed teeth are illustration controls, not measurements.
The almost-closed mouth and broad snout avoid the exaggerated open jaws of the
game-derived candidate rejected during research. No game mesh or alligator is used.

The model is intentionally a simple, legible anatomy/size illustration at small
explorer sizes. It does not document Lolong's individual markings, dentition,
age, injuries or exact body condition.

Sources opened 2026-10-16. No labels or alternative display name are embedded.
