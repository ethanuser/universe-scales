# Earth's Inner Core (measured-inner-core)

Earth's solid inner core at the radius of the Preliminary Reference Earth Model, 1,221.5 km (2,443 km across), cut open by a quarter to show the innermost inner core, a central region about 650 km in radius where seismic waves travel with a different directional dependence. Colors are illustrative of an iron-nickel solid near 5,000-6,000 K; the innermost-core boundary is a seismological estimate, not a sharp surface.

## Sources

- A. M. Dziewonski and D. L. Anderson (1981), Preliminary Reference Earth Model, Phys. Earth Planet. Inter. 25, 297: inner-core radius 1221.5 km.
- J. Stephenson, H. Tkalcic and M. Sambridge (2021), Evidence for the innermost inner core, JGR Solid Earth 126, e2020JB020545: radius about 650 km.

Built by `scripts/build_measured_models.py inner-core` (CC0). Geometry is set by the numbers above; nothing is scaled by eye.
