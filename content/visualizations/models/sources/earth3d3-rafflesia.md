# Rafflesia arnoldii flower

Original CC0 botanical illustration by Universe Scales, built offline with
`scripts/build_earth3d3_models.py rafflesia`. No external pixels or mesh are copied.

## Scientific basis

- [Kew species profile](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:316069-1/general-information):
  a flower can span 1 m; five fleshy red-brown lobes have pale spots; a central
  column and disk occupy the floral cup. Facts are used as constraints, not Kew's
  NC-licensed photography or prose as an asset.
- [Nikolov et al., 2013, PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3831985/):
  the diaphragm roofs an open floral chamber surrounding the central column/disk.
- [SIU Parasitic Plant Connection](https://parasiticplants.siu.edu/Rafflesiaceae/description.html)
  and [species photographs](https://parasiticplants.siu.edu/Rafflesiaceae/Raff.arn.page.html):
  the disk bears projecting processes. Images were consulted, not copied.

Only the maximum horizontal flower span is fixed at 1 m. The complete geometry
is uniformly scaled to that span. Lobe outline/thickness, chamber depth,
diaphragm opening, bracts, process count and original spot texture are schematic
illustration controls. This is neither a specimen nor a diagnostic taxonomic
model. No free-standing stem or leaves are invented. The host is omitted.

The resting pose exposes the flower's opening; rotation exposes its chamber and
thick lobes. No caveat labels or alternate display name are embedded.

Sources opened 2026-10-16. CPU previews are not WebGL verification.
