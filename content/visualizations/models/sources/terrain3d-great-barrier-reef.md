# Great Barrier Reef: elevation and map geometry

Actual Reef Authority reef polygons selected within the Queensland viewing aperture, in contrasting turquoise over sampled coastal terrain and NOAA ETOPO1 sea floor. These polygons are mapped reef footprints, not a live-coral census, park boundary or detailed 3D coral survey. Relief and bathymetry are exaggerated 8 times; the reef layer is a color overlay on that regional surface, not individually measured reef heights. Geographic horizontal coordinates and Earth curvature are retained. The 2,300 km catalog length refers to the broad reef system, not a straight end-to-end measurement of every polygon. Contains Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority 2023 data under CC BY 4.0; modified by selecting and rasterizing mapped reefs. No endorsement is implied. Horizontal coordinates are not normalized to the catalog size. Colors are illustrative. The opaque sides and flat underside are arbitrary display cuts, not real cliffs or geological strata. Mapzen / Tilezen; SRTM, GMTED2010 and United States 3DEP terrain courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey; ETOPO1 ocean bathymetry courtesy of NOAA; Natural Earth public-domain map data; adaptation by Universe Scales. Ocean depths are sampled explicitly from NOAA ETOPO1 (1 arc-minute), using Natural Earth coastlines to select ocean samples; coastlines and land/ocean source transitions are generalized.

## Pinned source and processing

`terrain3d/great-barrier-reef/provenance.json` records every tile URL, SHA-256, provider header, grid bounds and elevation range, plus the original vector download hashes. The compact `terrain.npz` stores 257 × 193 original sampled elevations and longitude/latitude. The mesh retains every 2 grid vertex along each axis (129 × 97), while the elevation-shading texture retains the full input grid. `vectors.geojson.gz` retains losslessly compressed source fragments independently. Global land is pinned as a derived crop mask, not distributed as a world dataset. Source grid spacing is a sampling choice, not a claim of DEM resolution. Small scenes use local east/up/south coordinates with spherical WGS84-radius distances; regional scenes use a spherical Earth of radius 6371.0088 km in a local tangent frame. Large-scene curvature is retained; no map projection rescales the footprint to fit the quantity. Geographic approximation differs slightly from ellipsoidal surveying. Vertical gain: **8×**. No extreme-point correction or procedural terrain is added.

Normal offline rebuild: `./venv/bin/python scripts/build_terrain3d_models.py great-barrier-reef`. Optional network source refresh: add `--prepare --cache /tmp/terrain3d`. Registration: `python3 scripts/register_model.py handoff/terrain3d/terrain3d-great-barrier-reef.entry.json content/visualizations/models/terrain3d-great-barrier-reef.glb`.
