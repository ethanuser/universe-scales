# ETOPO 2022 rights and citation

Source checked 2026-10-23: [NOAA ETOPO 2022 ISO metadata](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/metadata/published/NOAA/NESDIS/NGDC/MGG/DEM/iso/xml/etopo_2022.xml).
The metadata identifies the NOAA-produced data as outside US copyright and
applies the **CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication worldwide**. It requests
citation and says the grid is unsuitable for navigation. This is a data license,
not an assumption based solely on the agency's ownership of the website.

Citation: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (2022),
*ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model*,
[doi:10.25921/fd45-gt74](https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74), accessed 2026-10-23.

The extracted rights statements and hash of the original XML are preserved in
`etopo-rights.json`. The complete third-party XML remains an optional research
cache outside the repo. The release's contributing datasets and limitations are
described in [MacFerrin et al. (2025)](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/17/1835/2025/).

No navigation, newest-survey, or uniform local survey-resolution claim is made.
