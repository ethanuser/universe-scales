# XJET image rights

Checked 2026-10-23: [XJET image policy](https://hea-www.harvard.edu/XJET/image_policy.txt).
The archive provides downloadable scientific FITS images for public use. Its
submission policy requires contributors to ensure the image is in the public
domain with its owner's permission. It requests acknowledgement of the original
observer and image contributor. It does not impose a noncommercial restriction.

Credit for this image: NASA/ESA Hubble Space Telescope, WFPC2; original principal
investigator W. Sparks; image contributed by E. Perlman;
Perlman et al. (1999), *Astronomical Journal* **117**, 2185; XJET archive.
The image descriptor is [m87_hst_f555w.txt](https://hea-www.harvard.edu/XJET/data/m87_hst_f555w.txt).
The original scientific array is linked directly from that archive's image page.

This is the archive's explicit public-domain/acknowledgement grant, not a claim
that XJET applies a Creative Commons license. No observer contact addresses or
download credentials are included in these notes.
