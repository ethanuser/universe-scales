# Mycoplasma cell schematic (mycoplasma-cell-schematic)

Original CC0 schematic built by scripts/build_mycoplasma_model.py (units nm): a wall-less, slightly flask-shaped cell 200 nm across and 260 nm long with a short attachment tip (typical of Mycoplasma genitalium), an 8 nm membrane, 70 ribosome glyphs of 20 nm (the size of a bacterial 70S ribosome) and a 3 nm coiled nucleoid. Counts and arrangement are illustrative.
