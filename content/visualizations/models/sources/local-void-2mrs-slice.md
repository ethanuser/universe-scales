# Local Void

Source: [Huchra et al. (2012), 2MASS Redshift Survey; Tully et al. (2019)](https://lweb.cfa.harvard.edu/~dfabricant/huchra/2mass/).

Scientific basis: [reference](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08329).

Actual 2MASS Redshift Survey galaxies in a 60 by 60 by 20 Mpc section through the Local Void's Aquila, Lacerta and Andromeda minima. Positions are barycentric redshift-space distances cz/H0, H0=75 km/s/Mpc, in supergalactic coordinates. The displayed window is a section through the void and surrounding galaxies, not its boundary or full volume. Flux limits, peculiar velocities and the Milky Way's zone of avoidance affect the apparent emptiness; missing survey regions must not be read as empty space. Every displayed point is a catalog galaxy; none were deleted to sculpt a cavity. Colors are for visibility, not observed galaxy colors.
