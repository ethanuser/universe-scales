# Great Pyramid of Giza (measured-great-pyramid)

The Great Pyramid as built, from survey measurements: a square base 230.33 m on a side (Petrie's mean of the four casing-base sides) and an original height of 146.6 m, giving the measured face slope of about 51.8 degrees. Faces show the smooth Tura-limestone casing that has since been stripped; the capstone, the slight concavity of the faces (under 1 m) and the present-day stepped core are not modeled.

## Sources

- W. M. Flinders Petrie (1883), The Pyramids and Temples of Gizeh, chapter 7: casing base sides 230.25-230.45 m (mean 230.33 m), slope 51 deg 52 min.
- M. Lehner and Z. Hawass (2017), Giza and the Pyramids: original height 146.6 m (280 royal cubits).

Built by `scripts/build_measured_models.py great-pyramid` (CC0). Geometry is set by the numbers above; nothing is scaled by eye.
