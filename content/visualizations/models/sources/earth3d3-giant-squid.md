# Giant squid: Architeuthis

Original CC0 anatomical illustration by Universe Scales. Rebuild offline:
`./venv/bin/python scripts/build_earth3d3_models.py squid`.

## Scientific basis

- [Smithsonian Ocean: Giant squid](https://ocean.si.edu/ocean-life/invertebrates/giant-squid),
  anatomy and size sections: eight arms, two feeding tentacles, distal clubs,
  large lateral eyes, mantle and funnel. It reports 13 m total length, a 2.25 m
  maximum recorded mantle length and a posterior-tip-to-arm-tip span rarely
  exceeding 5 m. Tentacle extension accounts for much of total length.
- [Smithsonian NMNH](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/explore/giant-squid):
  anatomical diagram and preserved specimens, consulted rather than copied.
- [Natural History Museum](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/giant-and-colossal-squid-revealing-secrets-largest-invertebrates.html):
  approximately 13 m for large females and 10 m for males; giant and colossal
  squid have different body plans.

The displayed posterior-tip-to-club span is 13 m. The illustrated mantle is
2.25 m long; the eight shorter arms end within a 5 m axial body-and-arm span.
These are constraints for an authored example, not dimensions of one specimen.
The intermediate contours, eye shapes, arm poses, sucker dimensions/counts,
fin contours and muted red display colors are authored. No colossal-squid hooks
are added. The long feeding tentacles are visibly extended.

The asset is a simplified anatomy/size illustration, not a scan, dissection,
life posture reconstruction or maximum-size certainty statement. Its small body
relative to the full 13 m extent is intentional. The source literature cautions
that postmortem stretching complicates total-length estimates.

Sources opened 2026-10-16. No external artwork or runtime dependencies.
