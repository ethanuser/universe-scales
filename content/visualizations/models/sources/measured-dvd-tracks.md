# DVD Track Pitch (measured-dvd-tracks)

Three tracks of a DVD data layer seen from above, at the standard 0.74 um track pitch. Pit and land lengths are whole multiples of the 0.133 um channel bit within the EFMPlus run-length limits (3T-11T and 14T), so the shortest pit is 0.40 um; the sequence is a deterministic random example, not real data. Pit width (~0.32 um) and depth (~105 nm, a quarter wavelength of 650 nm light in polycarbonate) are typical values; pits are drawn as boxes.

## Sources

- ECMA-267 (2001), 120 mm DVD Read-Only Disk: track pitch 0.74 um, channel bit length 0.133 um (4.7 GB), run lengths 3T-11T and 14T.

Built by `scripts/build_measured_models.py dvd-tracks` (CC0). Geometry is set by the numbers above; nothing is scaled by eye.
