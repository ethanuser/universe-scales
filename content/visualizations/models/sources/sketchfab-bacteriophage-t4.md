# Bacteriophage T4 (sketchfab-bacteriophage-t4)

[Virus (Bacteriophage T4)](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/virus-bacteriophage-t4-8e34ff590e1f42ef8f0bd20dd8e7a27a) by andresfranciscosanchez13, CC BY 4.0 (license checked via the Sketchfab API, 2026-10-16). Artistic model. Its display ground plane (node Object_4) was removed; gltf-transform 4.5.0 weld, simplify (ratio 0.3), prune, quantize. Calibrated so head top to tail tip (0.3231 model units) is the listed 200 nm; the six tail fibers reach about 13% further below.
