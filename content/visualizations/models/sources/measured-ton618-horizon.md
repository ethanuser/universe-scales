# TON 618 Event Horizon (measured-ton618-horizon)

The event horizon of the quasar TON 618's black hole for a mass of 4.07e10 suns: a Schwarzschild radius r = 2GM/c^2 of 1.2e14 m, so 2.4e14 m (1,600 au) across. Drawn to the same scale in front of it are Neptune's orbit (30 au) and the heliopause where Voyager 1 crossed it (121.6 au). The horizon is not a surface one could see; the mass estimate is uncertain by a factor of a few.

## Sources

- X. Ge et al. (2019), AJ 157, 148: virial mass of TON 618, 4.07e10 solar masses.
- Neptune's mean orbital radius 30.07 au (NASA); Voyager 1 crossed the heliopause at 121.6 au (Stone et al. 2013, Science 341, 150).

Built by `scripts/build_measured_models.py ton618-horizon` (CC0). Geometry is set by the numbers above; nothing is scaled by eye.
