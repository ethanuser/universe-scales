# Prelude FLNG

## Dimensional and arrangement basis

- [Shell Australia: Prelude overview](https://www.shell.com.au/about-us/what-we-do/gas-and-lng/prelude-flng-facility/prelude-overview.html):
  488 m overall length and 74 m width.
- [Shell: Floating LNG](https://www.shell.com/what-we-do/oil-and-natural-gas/liquefied-natural-gas-lng/floating-lng.html),
  construction-film transcripts: fourteen topside modules and stern living
  quarters. Images/footage establish only broad arrangement.
- [International Gas Union, 2015–2018 FLNG report, p. 43](https://www.datocms-assets.com/146580/1736938297-flng-report-2015-2018.pdf):
  hull envelope 488 × 74 × 42 m.

The full above/below-water hull is shown. It retains the sourced length, width
and depth. No operational draft/waterline is asserted. Two schematic rows of
seven modules represent the fourteen-module organization, not measured module
positions. Accommodation, process skids, pipes, flare lattice, turret, crane
geometry, hull sections, colors and all other details are authored approximations.
There is no equipment inventory or assertion that an individual vessel component
has those local dimensions. Mooring chains, underwater turret extent, risers,
internal tanks, thrusters and water are omitted.

This is an exterior size/organization illustration, not naval architecture,
process engineering, a safety model or an operational-state reconstruction.
The source's 488 m length is the calibration reference; no axis is stretched.
