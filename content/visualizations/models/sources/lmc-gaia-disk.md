# Large Magellanic Cloud

Source: [ESA/Gaia/DPAC; van der Marel & Cioni (2001); Pietrzynski et al. (2019)](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/).

Scientific basis: [reference](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0105339).

Gaia DR3 candidate LMC stars at their measured sky positions and observed BP-RP colors, intersected with the published 34.7-degree stellar disk plane at 49.59 kpc. Proper-motion and parallax cuts reduce Milky Way contamination. The plane is an ensemble distance approximation: individual stellar depths, disk thickness and gas are not measured here. The 9.86-kpc disk aperture follows this item's characteristic diameter; fainter outskirts continue beyond it. Brightness is compressed for display and colors are an ordered BP-RP palette. No invented stars or depth scatter.
