# Baryon Acoustic Oscillation Scale

Source: [Sloan Digital Sky Survey / BOSS](https://www.sdss4.org/dr17/).

Scientific basis: [reference](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0501171).

Actual BOSS galaxy redshifts from SDSS DR17 in a 150 by 150 by 40 Mpc comoving slice centered at RA=180 degrees, Dec=20 degrees, z=0.475. Positions use flat LCDM with H0=70 km/s/Mpc and Omega_m=0.27. This is a survey sample spanning the approximate BAO separation scale, not an individual acoustic shell or an identified BAO structure. The BAO excess requires a statistical pair-correlation measurement across a much larger sample and cannot be inferred by eye from this patch. Colors encode redshift, not galaxy color. All points come from the catalog; no patterns are imposed.
