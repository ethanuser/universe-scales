# Andromeda Galaxy disk (andromeda-galaxy-disk)

Image: https://noirlab.edu/public/images/noao-m31fsqblock/ (CC BY 4.0). Credit: KPNO/NOIRLab/NSF/AURA/
Adam Block. 162.24 x 58.90 arcmin; at 783 kpc (McConnachie 2012) about 37 x 13 kpc.
scripts/build_nebula_volumes.py andromeda: stars removed (foreground Milky Way stars), gas split into
smooth glow and fine structure (scripts/nebula_volume.py, front view calibrated to the photo).
Depth: a disk inclined 77 degrees about the photo's fitted major axis (z = b tan i, b = projected
minor-axis offset), Laplace thickness 0.35 kpc, plus a round bulge (Gaussian, 2.2 kpc) at the center.
The face-on appearance is the photo deprojected, so it is smeared along the minor axis; which edge
is near is not encoded; the satellites M32 and M110 in the photo are placed on the disk plane.
