# Kola Superdeep Borehole (measured-kola-borehole)

A slice of the rock column drilled by borehole SG-3, to its final depth of 12,262 m (1989). The upper 6,842 m are Proterozoic volcanic and sedimentary rocks of the Pechenga complex; below lie Archean gneisses, granites and amphibolites. The bottom-hole temperature was about 180 C. The hole itself is about 0.2 m wide and is drawn 40 m wide to be visible; the slab's width is arbitrary context.

## Sources

- Ye. A. Kozlovsky (ed., 1984/1987), The Superdeep Well of the Kola Peninsula, Springer: Proterozoic/Archean contact at 6,842 m.
- Final depth 12,262 m (1989) and ~180 C bottom-hole temperature: summarized by the Kola SG-3 project reports and Wikipedia's cited sources.

Built by `scripts/build_measured_models.py kola-borehole` (CC0). Geometry is set by the numbers above; nothing is scaled by eye.
