# Angular machine scenes

These five scenes are original procedural illustrations, not scans or models of branded products. They show the component that rotates separately from the stationary support: a perforated front-loading washer drum, a stack of magnetic disks on an HDD spindle, a record and platter, a spoked wheel around a fixed axle, and a blender blade assembly beneath a cutaway transparent pitcher. Dimensions are scene-scale design choices for readable model staging, not specifications for a particular product.

Reference material was used only to check familiar component relationships and terminology:

- [Seagate ATA Product Manual (1621PMA)](https://www.seagate.com/support/disc/manuals/ata/1621pma.pdf), for disk/platter and drive assembly context.
- [Audio-Technica AT-LPA2 User Manual](https://docs.audio-technica.com/eu/AT-LPA2-UM-EN.pdf), for turntable, record and tonearm arrangement.
- [Park Tool: How Wheel Truing Works](https://www.parktool.com/en-us/blog/repair-help/how-wheel-truing-works), for tire, rim, hub, spoke and nipple relationships.
- [Vitamix 48-ounce container resources](https://www.vitamix.com/us/en_us/shop/48-ounce-container), linking its Container Use and Care manual, for a jar-mounted blade assembly context.
- [Bosch washer owner support](https://www.bosch-home.com/us/owner-support/get-support/washers/), for front-loading washer and drum context.

The washer's visible perforations, tub cutaway, HDD head positioning, tonearm, wheel lacing, and blender blade shape are simplified geometry. No performance, internal tolerances, or exact branded construction is claimed.
