# Blu-ray Track Pitch (measured-bluray-tracks)

Three tracks of a Blu-ray data layer seen from above, at the standard 0.32 um track pitch. Pit and land lengths are whole multiples of the 74.5 nm channel bit (25 GB layer) within the 1-7PP run-length limits (2T-8T), so the shortest pit is 0.149 um; the sequence is a deterministic random example, not real data. Pit width (~0.14 um) and depth (~65 nm) are typical values; pits are drawn as boxes.

## Sources

- Blu-ray Disc Association, BD-ROM format white paper: track pitch 0.32 um, channel bit length 74.5 nm (25 GB), 1-7PP modulation (run lengths 2T-8T).

Built by `scripts/build_measured_models.py bluray-tracks` (CC0). Geometry is set by the numbers above; nothing is scaled by eye.
