# Crab and Ring nebulae: observed light, published 3D shapes

Both use scripts/build_nebula_volumes.py (photo -> calibrated glowing sprites, see
scripts/nebula_volume.py), so the front view reproduces the photograph.

## Crab Nebula (crab-nebula-volume)

Image: https://esahubble.org/images/heic0515a/ (CC BY 4.0). Credit: NASA, ESA and Allison Loll/Jeff
Hester (Arizona State University); acknowledgement Davide De Martin (ESA/Hubble). 6.41 arcmin square,
north up; at about 6,500 ly the field is 12.1 ly (listed size 11 ly).
Depth: Martin, Milisavljevic & Drout 2021 (MNRAS 502, 1864; https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stab2848)
mapped the filaments in 3D from their Doppler shifts: they form a roughly ellipsoidal shell around
the synchrotron nebula. Here a prolate shell is fitted to the photo's brightness moments (semi-axes
5.2 x 3.8 ly, long axis assumed in the sky plane); filaments (fine structure) go on it, near or far
side chosen by a smooth random field; the smooth bluish synchrotron glow fills the interior. That
placement is illustrative, not the published per-filament map.

## Ring Nebula (ring-nebula-volume)

Image: https://esawebb.org/images/weic2320b/ (CC BY 4.0). Credit: ESA/Webb, NASA, CSA, M. Barlow,
N. Cox, R. Wesson. NIRCam, 2.14 x 2.17 arcmin; the central 78% is used (about 1.25 ly at 2,567 ly,
Gaia's 787 pc). The listed 1 ly spans the main ring.
Depth: O'Dell, Ferland, Henney & Peimbert 2013 (AJ 145, 92; https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-6256/145/4/92)
describe the main ring as a barrel-like shell seen nearly end-on. Here the ring (fitted ellipse,
semi-axes 0.40 x 0.35 ly) is the barrel's waist, the faint center lies on end caps 1.6 times the
ring radius along the line of sight, and the outer halo fills a larger shell. Illustrative placement.
