# San Andreas Fault: elevation and map geometry

USGS Quaternary Fault and Fold Database traces whose fault_name contains San Andreas, with original section identities and gaps retained. Line geometry is simplified with a 300 m horizontal tolerance for this regional display; original vertices and fragments are pinned in the source. The bright trace is a widened cartographic symbol, not a chasm or measured fault-zone width. Relief is exaggerated 5 times. The source includes multiple mapped strands; their aggregate linework is not a single traversable route. The separate approximate 1,210 km ruler represents the catalog system length and is not used to rescale the coordinates. Horizontal coordinates are not normalized to the catalog size. Colors are illustrative. The opaque sides and flat underside are arbitrary display cuts, not real cliffs or geological strata. Mapzen / Tilezen; SRTM, GMTED2010 and United States 3DEP terrain courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey; ETOPO1 ocean bathymetry courtesy of NOAA; Natural Earth public-domain map data; adaptation by Universe Scales. Ocean depths are sampled explicitly from NOAA ETOPO1 (1 arc-minute), using Natural Earth coastlines to select ocean samples; coastlines and land/ocean source transitions are generalized.

## Pinned source and processing

`terrain3d/san-andreas/provenance.json` records every tile URL, SHA-256, provider header, grid bounds and elevation range, plus the original vector download hashes. The compact `terrain.npz` stores 161 × 129 original sampled elevations and longitude/latitude. The mesh retains every 2 grid vertex along each axis (81 × 65), while the elevation-shading texture retains the full input grid. `vectors.geojson.gz` retains losslessly compressed source fragments independently. Global land is pinned as a derived crop mask, not distributed as a world dataset. Source grid spacing is a sampling choice, not a claim of DEM resolution. Small scenes use local east/up/south coordinates with spherical WGS84-radius distances; regional scenes use a spherical Earth of radius 6371.0088 km in a local tangent frame. Large-scene curvature is retained; no map projection rescales the footprint to fit the quantity. Geographic approximation differs slightly from ellipsoidal surveying. Vertical gain: **5×**. No extreme-point correction or procedural terrain is added.

Normal offline rebuild: `./venv/bin/python scripts/build_terrain3d_models.py san-andreas`. Optional network source refresh: add `--prepare --cache /tmp/terrain3d`. Registration: `python3 scripts/register_model.py handoff/terrain3d/terrain3d-san-andreas.entry.json content/visualizations/models/terrain3d-san-andreas.glb`.
