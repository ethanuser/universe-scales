# Ever Given — original dimensioned exterior illustration

Original geometry, colors and corrugation texture by Universe Scales, **CC0 1.0**.
No third-party mesh, vessel photo, plan pixels or logos are embedded. Offline
builder: `scripts/build_earth3d2_ship.py`.

## Opened sources (2026-10-16)

- [Panama Maritime Authority, R-026-2021-DIAM, *Grounding of MV Ever Given*
  (2023 report; gCaptain-hosted copy)](https://gcaptain.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Final-Investigation-Report-Ever-Given-23-March-2021.pdf):
  report pages **9–11**, Table 1, Figure 2. Overall length **399.98 m**,
  moulded beam **58.80 m**, moulded depth **32.90 m**. The general-arrangement
  plan was visually inspected to place the accommodation forward of midships
  and the funnel aft. These locations are approximate, not digitized measurements.
- [Container Trader, 40-foot high cube](https://www.containertrader.eu/en/containers/40-high-cube):
  nominal external envelope **12.192 × 2.438 × 2.896 m**. This secondary
  supplier's product specification provides the illustrative boxes' envelope;
  it does not establish the actual containers aboard Ever Given.

The report's 399.98 m differs from the unchanged catalog's 399.94 m by 4 cm
(about 0.01%). Calibration uses **399.98 model metres** and uniformly maps it
to the existing catalog value. The beam and all details scale together.
No independent-axis stretch or silent dataset change is made.

## Representation limits

Hull sections, keel/bilge/bow/transom curves, superstructure dimensions,
railings/antennae, windows, machinery, paint and texture are authored exterior
simplifications. This is not a scan, detailed shipyard model, stability model
or claim about the ship's current registration, modifications or loading.

A deterministic **partial illustrative cargo load** uses 40-foot high-cube
boxes. It is not the stowage plan, full 20,124 TEU capacity or a reconstruction
of the March 2021 grounding. Tier counts, small gaps and color arrangement are
display choices. No container identifiers, carrier logos or personal data.
The propeller, rudder, below-deck holds, interior, water and canal are omitted.

The report is used only for factual geometry and approximate arrangement.
Its pages and artwork are not redistributed. Temporary rendered pages were
inspected under `/tmp/earth3d2/ever-given-page-*.png`. The final model has
its own original texture. CPU rest/quarter-camera previews are in the handoff;
production WebGL, explorer and mobile review remain for the lead.
