# Bootes Void

Source: [Sloan Digital Sky Survey; Kirshner et al. (1987)](https://www.sdss4.org/dr17/).

Scientific basis: [reference](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1987ApJ...314..493K).

SDSS DR17 spectroscopic galaxies in a 150 by 150 by 40 Mpc slice centered on the published Bootes Void position and cz=15,500 km/s. The historical H0=50 km/s/Mpc convention is retained because this item's 124-Mpc diameter comes from that convention; adopting H0=70 changes that diameter to about 89 Mpc. Catalog redshifts are converted using cz/H0 without a peculiar-velocity correction. The plotted slice includes the sparse center and surrounding galaxies; it does not define membership or a spherical boundary. Survey incompleteness remains. Colors encode redshift, not galaxy color, and every point is an actual catalog location.
