  https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso1907a/ credits **EHT Collaboration**.
  https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/ releases its images under
  **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**, unless individually noted.
  No different license is shown on this image. The original color image is
  embedded in a cropped, feathered flat aperture; that modification is disclosed
  in the registry note and source note. No generated image pixels replace data.
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Radcliffe Wave cloud coordinates:** Alves et al. (2020), Nature 578, 237–239,
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1874-z. Its interactive supplementary figure
  identifies 141 cloud points as members. Only their factual names/coordinates are
  extracted; Plotly code, article prose/figures and posterior draws are excluded.
  The article identifies https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/07L7YZ as the major-cloud
  database. Its DOI metadata explicitly records **CC0 1.0**:
  https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.7910/DVN/07L7YZ. A compact rights record is
  pinned in `../sources/space3d3/radcliffe-data-rights.json`.
  Direct Harvard Dataverse API access returned HTTP 403 in this session; the
  public article's numerical supplement and DataCite metadata were accessible.
- **NASA textures and Horizons vectors:** existing credited NASA model maps and
  NASA/JPL geometric ephemerides, under the repository's NASA usage guidelines.
  Existing asset identities/hashes are recorded with each derived entry.
- **Original diagrams:** CC0. Published orbital elements, measured distances and
  mathematical definitions are cited numerical facts, not redistributed article
  text, figures, tables-as-artwork, or source software.
