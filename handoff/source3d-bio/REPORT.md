# Dust Mite source review

Rejected: the reviewed `paumarti` model is a generalized cartoon and its full span, including appendages, does not calibrate the requested body length. No model is proposed or registered.

## Sources checked

- [Dust Mite by paumarti](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dust-mite-29ea8fe6ca89488db3affe730818b59d) — public API: `https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/29ea8fe6ca89488db3affe730818b59d`; downloadable, CC BY 4.0 (`https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`). Author describes it as a “cute mite”; no species or specimen provenance. Rejected after visual review for stylization and body-length mismatch.
- [Dust mite by shawnsolus](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dust-mite-38d385d956044fa3a2e9d71bfa7d4e8c) — public API: `https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/38d385d956044fa3a2e9d71bfa7d4e8c`; downloadable, CC BY 4.0. Trnio capture is fragmented with substantial substrate/scan artifacts; no specimen or species provenance. Rejected.

## Research scope

Reviewed both search candidates, their public API metadata/licenses and staged geometry/previews. A bounded Sketchfab API search covered `house dust mite`, `Dermatophagoides`, and `mite scan`; no additional small CC BY/CC0 candidates appeared on the first result page. No anti-bot restrictions were bypassed. Research stopped here; no further search or substitute geometry is proposed.
