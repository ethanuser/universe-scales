# source3d-hail — NCAR Vivian lead rejected

The exact lead is NSF NCAR's [Hailstones AR page](https://ncar.github.io/psif2020ar/pages/hail.html),
which embeds [`VivianHailModel.glb`](https://ncar.github.io/psif2020ar/VivianHailModel.glb).
NCAR CISL's [education resources page](https://www.cisl.ucar.edu/outreach/resources)
identifies the AR hailstone as the record stone from Vivian, South Dakota. The
provided [`NCAR/psif2020ar` GitHub repository](https://github.com/NCAR/psif2020ar)
currently returns 404; the official NCAR-hosted page and model URL are live.

**Rejected: licensing.** UCAR's [Website Terms of Use](https://www.ucar.edu/terms-of-use/website)
grant use of Site materials for “non-commercial, non-profit research or
educational purposes only.” The page footer carries `© UCAR`, and the terms
require the copyright notice and source citation to accompany copies. This is
purpose-limited, noncommercial permission, not CC BY, CC0, public domain, or a
clear general redistribution grant. It fails the owner's explicit
CC BY/CC0/public-domain import policy. Do not bundle, redistribute, or register
this model. No NCAR source/model files, derived GLB, builder, test, or proposed
registry entry are retained in the repository.

Scientific caveat if this lead is reconsidered after the owner obtains a
qualifying license: [Shedd et al. (2021), “Hailstone shapes”](https://doi.org/10.1175/JAS-D-20-0250.1)
reports that the Vivian 3D scan came from a plaster cast whose shape had
deteriorated relative to the actual stone. This is not a faithful recovered
ice surface.
